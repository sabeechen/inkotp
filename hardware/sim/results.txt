   firmware takes 0.6 s to raise HOLD: 0.05 s drops out; 0.20 s drops out; 0.55 s drops out; 0.65 s stays on; 0.80 s stays on

   off after 7 to 11 ms, and stays off

   held 1.0 s: depends on the parts (4 of 8 corners stay on)
   held 1.5 s: depends on the parts (4 of 8 corners stay on)
   held 2.0 s: switches off
   held 3.0 s: switches off

   threshold 0.8 V: 1.6 s
   threshold 1.3 V: 0.9 s
   threshold 1.5 V: 0.7 s

   plugging in with no press: comes on; reset held 3 s: rail stays up, running again afterwards
   firmware lets go while plugged in: stays on; after unplugging: off
   unplugged while firmware holds on: stays on
